Government payroll is the definition of a system that cannot fail: every state employee,
every pay period, under rules that have accumulated for decades on mainframe
infrastructure. Modernizing it is digital government at its least glamorous and most
consequential.

## The problem

A statewide HR and payroll system running on mainframe technology — expensive to
operate, hard to staff for, and difficult to extend — needed a complete refactor to
cloud-enabled infrastructure without disrupting the paychecks it exists to deliver.

## The approach

Serving as lead data scientist on the refactor: mapping the legacy system's data and
rules, designing the modern data architecture that replaces it, and applying modern data
principles — pipelines, lineage, measurement — so the new system is not just hosted
differently but genuinely more observable and maintainable.
