## Skill List

### Data Architecture

- **Tier:** Master
- **Description:** One does not achieve mastery by studying clean schemas — it is earned by surviving the cursed ones. After years of confronting legacy dungeons and untamed data wilds, the wielder now bends structure itself to purpose, designing vaults of information that endure long after the original architects have departed.

### System Design

- **Tier:** Expert
- **Description:** Hardened by battles against scale, chaos, and the dreaded 3 AM page. Architects solutions that hold their shape under siege — services decoupled, failures contained, and complexity tamed before it consumes the realm.

### Backend / API Design

- **Tier:** Expert
- **Description:** Forged through countless expeditions into server-side citadels. Contracts are written with care, endpoints respond with purpose, and the chaos of distributed systems is kept firmly behind the gate.

### Technical Leadership

- **Tier:** Expert
- **Description:** Seasoned in the art of rallying a party — aligning vision, clearing blockers, and ensuring no guild member is left to fight the dragon alone. Reviews sharpen the blade; mentorship forges the wielder.

### Infrastructure

- **Tier:** Adept
- **Description:** Comfortable navigating the cloud kingdoms and the pipelines that sustain them. Provisions environments with intention and keeps the machinery of deployment humming so that others may focus on the quest.

### System Auditing

- **Tier:** Expert
- **Description:** Called upon when the realm suspects something has gone wrong beneath the surface. Traces anomalies through logs and lineage, surfaces hidden truths, and delivers verdicts that guide the path forward.
